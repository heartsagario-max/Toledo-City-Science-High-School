A public science high school where curious students learn by experimenting, building, and solving real problems.

Our program joins science, technology, engineering, and mathematics so every learner leaves ready for college, careers, and their community.

Our story

Toledo City Science High School belongs to the Schools Division of Toledo City, in Cebu, Philippines. Our seal carries the year 1996, the year we began.

Since then the school has focused on learners with a strong interest in science and mathematics. The four sections of our seal show the heart of the curriculum: the microscope for science, the circuit for technology, the hard hat for engineering, and the graph for mathematics.

How admission works

Getting in takes three steps. Staying in takes steady effort.

  1. Submit your application

    Fill out the application form and send the required documents to the school office.

  2. Pass the entrance exam

    Applicants take a written exam covering science and mathematics. Only those who pass move on.

  3. Pitch your project

    Applicants who pass the exam present a project idea to a panel of teachers and explain how they would build it.

  4. Keep a grade of 88 or above

    Once admitted, students must maintain a general average of 88 or higher to stay in the program.

Exam dates, required documents, and pitching guidelines are set by the school. Contact the office to confirm them.
